# Water Supply

Department: Water Department

No water supply, water not coming, pani nahi aa raha, paani band, tap water not coming,
low water pressure, pipeline burst, water leakage, pipe leak, damaged water line,
jal supply band, nal se pani nahi, water connection problem, no drinking water.

No water supply for multiple days is categorized as Water Supply and is HIGH priority
when it affects a house, locality or society. Pipeline burst or major leakage is HIGH priority.
