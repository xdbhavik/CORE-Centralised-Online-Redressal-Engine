# Power Supply

Department: Electricity Department

Power outage, power cut, no electricity, no light, light not coming, light not working,
bijli nahi aa rahi, bijli band, bijli cut, light nahi, batti nahi, batti band,
electricity cut, current not coming, meter not working, transformer fault, transformer blast,
exposed wire, sparking wire, broken electricity pole, voltage fluctuation.

No electricity in a house or locality for hours or days is categorized as Power Supply.
Power outage for many days (3 days, 10 days, das din) is HIGH priority.
Exposed wires, sparking or transformer faults are dangerous and HIGH priority.
