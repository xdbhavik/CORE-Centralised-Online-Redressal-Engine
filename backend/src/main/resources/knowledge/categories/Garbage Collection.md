# Garbage Collection

Department: Sanitation Department

Garbage not collected, kachra nahi uthaya, kachra collection, waste dumping, trash pile,
dustbin overflowing, garbage truck not coming, safai nahi hoti, dirty locality,
waste on road, dead animal removal, sweeping not done, sanitation problem.

Garbage not collected for days or waste dumped on roads is categorized as Garbage Collection.
Garbage pileup causing smell or health risk in a locality is HIGH priority.
