# Drainage

Department: Sanitation Department

Drainage blockage, drain blocked, nali band, naali jam, sewer overflow, gutter overflow,
drain water on road, manhole open, manhole cover missing, sewage leak, gatar ka pani,
water logging, stagnant water, drain smell, drainage repair.

Drainage blockage, sewer overflow or open manholes are categorized as Drainage.
Sewer overflow or open manhole near houses or roads is HIGH priority and dangerous.
