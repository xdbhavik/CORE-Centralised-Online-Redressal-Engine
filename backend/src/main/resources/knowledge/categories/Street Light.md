# Street Light

Department: Electricity Department

Street light not working, streetlight off, lamp post broken, light pole damaged,
street light band, sheri light nahi, road light not working, public light off,
pole light fused, dark street at night, street light repair.

A street light or lamp post that is not working on a public road is categorized as Street Light.
Street light complaints are usually MEDIUM priority unless the pole or wire is dangerous.
