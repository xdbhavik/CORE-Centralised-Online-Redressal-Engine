# Road Department

Handles potholes, damaged roads, unsafe road surface, broken footpaths, road repair requests and damaged bridges.

Common citizen phrases: rasta kharab, raasta kharab, sadak kharab, road damaged, gaddha,
pothole on road, broken road, road repair, footpath broken, bridge damaged, pul kharab,
road cave in, cracked road, accident due to bad road.

Potholes and damaged road surfaces should be categorized as Road Repair.
Damaged or broken bridges are also categorized as Road Repair and are HIGH priority when unsafe.
