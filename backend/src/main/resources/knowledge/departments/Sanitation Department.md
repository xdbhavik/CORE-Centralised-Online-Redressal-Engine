# Sanitation Department

Handles garbage collection, waste dumping, drainage blockage, sewer overflow and public sanitation complaints.

Common citizen phrases: kachra nahi uthaya, garbage not collected, dustbin overflowing,
safai nahi hoti, waste on road, nali band, naali jam, drainage blocked, sewer overflow,
gatar ka pani, manhole open, water logging, drain smell.

Garbage not collected should be categorized as Garbage Collection. Drainage blockage or sewer overflow should be categorized as Drainage.

Sewer overflow, open manholes and long-pending garbage pileup are HIGH priority.
