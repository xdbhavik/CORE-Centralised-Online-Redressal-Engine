# Water Department

Handles citizen complaints about no water supply, low water pressure, dirty water, pipeline burst, water leakage and damaged public water lines.

Common citizen phrases: pani nahi aa raha, paani band, no water supply, tap water not coming,
jal nahi aa raha, pipe leak, pipeline burst, water leakage, low pressure water,
dirty water, ganda pani, contaminated water, yellow water, smelly water.

No water supply for multiple days should be categorized as Water Supply and can be high priority when it affects a locality.

Dirty water or contaminated water should be categorized as Water Quality and treated as high priority.
