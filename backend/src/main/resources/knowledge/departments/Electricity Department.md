# Electricity Department

Handles power outage, transformer fault, broken electricity pole, exposed electrical wire and street light complaints.

Common citizen phrases: no electricity, no light, light not coming, light not working,
power cut, electricity cut, bijli nahi aa rahi, bijli band, light nahi, batti nahi,
current nahi aa raha, meter problem, voltage fluctuation, sparking wire.

Power outage affecting a house or locality is categorized as Power Supply.
Power outage lasting many days (3 days, 10 days, das din) is HIGH priority.
Street light not working on a public road is categorized as Street Light.
Exposed wires, sparking or transformer damage are dangerous and HIGH priority.
